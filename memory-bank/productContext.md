# Product Context for RovVision2_Sonar

## Purpose
RovVision2_Sonar exists to provide researchers, explorers, and underwater operators with a comprehensive system for remotely controlling underwater vehicles while receiving high-quality visual feedback through both traditional cameras and sonar imaging. This system enables underwater exploration, inspection, and research in environments that would be dangerous or impossible for human divers.

## Problems Solved

### Safety
- Allows exploration of deep, hazardous, or contaminated underwater environments without risking human safety
- Enables operation in extreme conditions (temperature, pressure, visibility) that would be dangerous for divers

### Accessibility
- Makes underwater research accessible to a broader range of scientists who aren't trained divers
- Enables longer-duration underwater operations than would be possible with human divers
- Allows exploration of deep areas beyond human diving capabilities

### Data Quality
- Provides stable, consistent imaging through stereo vision for accurate underwater measurements
- Incorporates sonar capabilities for navigation and imaging in low-visibility conditions
- Enables systematic documentation of underwater environments

### Control Precision
- Offers precise control mechanisms including position, depth, and attitude hold features
- Provides multiple control interfaces (joystick, UI controls) for different operator preferences
- Implements PID control systems for stable hovering and movement

### Data Management
- Records synchronized video and sensor data for post-mission analysis
- Allows real-time monitoring of system status, sensor readings, and environmental conditions
- Supports data transmission with optimized compression for underwater communication constraints

## User Experience

### Primary Users
- Marine researchers and scientists
- Underwater archaeologists
- Industrial inspectors (offshore oil, underwater infrastructure)
- Search and rescue operations
- Educational institutions

### Key User Needs
1. **Intuitive Control**: Simple interface allowing focus on the mission rather than vehicle operation
2. **Reliable Visual Feedback**: Clear, real-time video with minimal latency
3. **Stability Assistance**: Automated holds for depth, heading, and position
4. **System Awareness**: Comprehensive telemetry and status information
5. **Data Recording**: Ability to document discoveries and findings
6. **Mission Planning**: Tools to plan and execute underwater operations efficiently

## Future Direction
The system continues to evolve with a focus on improving sonar integration, enhancing control systems, and developing more autonomous capabilities to further expand the utility of ROVs in underwater research, inspection, and exploration.
